Post-Checkout Drawer Lock (DEF-033) Enforced

Once a child is checked out (`check_out_at != null`), Caregiver daily care inputs lock immediately. Edits require an Admin/Supervisor override key + reason logged to `core_audit_log`.

24-Hour Attendance Edit Lock (BR-03-01) Mandatory Rule

Records older than 24 hours reject `Teacher`/`CareGiver` edits with HTTP 422 (`BR-03-01`). Only `Principal` or `Owner` may apply audited overrides.
