## 2. Objective of the Entire Project

Build a **multi-tenant School & Early-Years Daycare CRM operations platform** (Django + Django Ninja + PostgreSQL backend, Next.js App Router frontend) that unifies admissions, curriculum and assessments, classroom and toddler daily-care attendance, child health and safeguarding, fee billing and Stripe Checkout payments, HR/compliance/payroll, vendor procurement, school events, emergency communications, and executive governance into one secure platform.

### Core Project Objectives & Success Outcomes
- **Strict Multi-Tenant & Role Isolation:** Guarantee zero cross-tenant or unauthorized role/object data disclosure via JWT RBAC, object-level guards, and forced PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (`NOBYPASSRLS`).
- **Operational & Safeguarding Compliance:** Enforce real-world school policies at the Business Object layer (`rule_registry.md`) — including 24-hour attendance locks, mandatory parental medication consent (`BR-07-01`), 1-hour incident parent SLAs with Principal escalation, DBS staff compliance gates, and 3-way procurement matching.
- **Financial Integrity:** Process fee schedules, late fees, tiered waivers/refunds, and online parent payments with decimal arithmetic, transactional rollback on bulk import errors, and Stripe webhook-only payment confirmation (no raw card storage on school servers).
- **Accessible Role-Specific UX & Performance:** Deliver fast, responsive, Oil Pastel / token-driven portals for every school persona targeting `<200 ms` p95 API latency with full automated test coverage (`pytest` + Playwright E2E).

### Out of Scope
- Replacing the Django / Django Ninja / PostgreSQL backend or Next.js App Router frontend.
- Using browser session emulation as production authentication or storing raw payment card details on school servers.
- Automated AI educational or safeguarding decisions without an approved human-review workflow.
